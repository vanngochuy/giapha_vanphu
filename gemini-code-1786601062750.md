# Frontend UI/UX & Aesthetic Rules

## 1. Aesthetic Concept: Classic Vintage & Mystical
Do NOT use standard corporate, bright, flat UI themes. The family tree visualization must embody a classic vintage, slightly mystical, and deep aesthetic to honor the ancestral history.
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Backgrounds: Deep, dark tones (e.g., dark teal, obsidian green, or deep charcoal).
  - Accents: Antique gold, muted bronze, or parchment colors for borders and text.
  - Node backgrounds: Subtle dark gradients with classic borders.
- **Typography:** Serif fonts for names and titles (e.g., Playfair Display, Merriweather) to give a historical, respectful feel. Sans-serif for small metadata (dates, locations).

## 2. Interaction Design
- **Canvas:** The D3.js/FamilyTreeJS canvas must take up at least 85% of the viewport.
- **Node Click:** Clicking a node should NOT open a pop-up modal. It should trigger a smooth side-panel sliding in from the right, displaying the full biography, death anniversary (Lunar date), and burial location.
- **Animations:** Slow, smooth transitions when expanding or collapsing family branches to maintain the calm, mystical feel of the interface.

## 3. Responsive Constraints
Ensure CSS is mobile-responsive. On small screens, the side-panel should slide up from the bottom instead of the right.
